Class PvP profiles · snapshot 2026-09-19

Per-class PvP profiles — published mechanics only

Nine profiles, one per class, written to answer a single question: what has actually been published about this class that matters in a battleground? Each line is a mechanic Blizzard stated or a reputable guide site reported from the BlizzCon build, with a link. Nothing here ranks classes — that cannot be done honestly yet, and the reason is printed below rather than hidden.

Read this before the profiles.

These are PvP-only profiles, per the project's stated focus. PvE detail appears only where it changes gearing or is the source of the ability being described. Two hard limits apply to every profile:

  • Blizzard has published a full class changelist for one class of nine (Paladin). Every other class rests on Icy Veins' reporting from the BlizzCon demo build, which those guides explicitly mark as subject to change. Blizzard Deep Dive
  • The beta is capped at level 20, rising to 30, and there is no rated PvP. No class has been played at level 60 in a battleground by anyone, so no strength claim about any class is verifiable. Wowhead beta guide

How to read a profile

What each profile contains

  • Battleground-relevant published changes — only mechanics that affect mobility, control, control-breaks, survivability, burst or objective play.
  • Evidence chip on every row: Official means Blizzard's own words; Guide means Icy Veins reported it from the BlizzCon build.
  • Race options as documented, with the racials that have a stated PvP use.
  • Open question — the specific thing about this class nobody can answer yet.

Pre-beta warning, on every row

These profiles rest on BlizzCon-build material: the panel changelists, Wowhead's talent calculator (demo data, not a beta extraction) and guide reporting. Blizzard has said the beta is where class balance is adjusted, so treat every row as provisional. IGN NordicTalent calculator The Beta observation log tracks which rows could be upgraded, and what an upgrade would require.

What no profile contains

  • A tier, grade, score or ranking of any kind.
  • Damage numbers, time-to-kill, or win rates.
  • Best-in-slot gear (no level-60 item data exists — see Gear).
  • Talent builds presented as optimal. Where a build is mentioned it is named as the source's sketch.
  • Arena advice. Arenas have not been announced for Forever.

Dated check, 19 September: even Icy Veins has not published a Forever PvP ranking yet — its tier-list directory (added 16 September) still marks the DPS and Healer PvP tier lists, and all three raiding lists, coming soon. When a reputable site publishes one, it will be linked here as that site's attributed analysis, not adopted as fact (C181). Icy Veins tier-list directory

Is there a best PvP class today? The honest answer

This is the question the site exists to answer, so it gets a straight reply rather than a hedge: no ranking can be published yet, and the reason is checkable. The three sources the brief names by hand were re-opened on 19 September 2026, and here is exactly what each carries for Forever PvP.

What the sites that do rank are doing. In the same week, boosting- and gold-adjacent domains published confident Forever PvP tier lists that disagree with each other — three different top-tier sets — for a beta that stops well short of level 60 and a game with no rated bracket (C205). Those pages are recorded as flagged examples and are never linked, because a contradiction sold as a ranking is exactly what this project refuses to reproduce.

What can be said today

  • Published mechanics per class — every battleground-relevant ability, talent and racial change Blizzard or a reputable outlet has documented, with a source link per line, in the nine profiles below.
  • Objective fit — which toolkits interact with capture points, flag carrying and reinforcements, stated as our reasoning and labelled as such (C171).
  • Race and racial changes — a reworked racial set with no immunities carried forward, which changes battleground play independently of class (C031, C046).
  • The structural facts — no rated PvP, a 14-rank seasonal Honor track, battlegrounds shared across rulesets (C020, C021, C025).

The rule for upgrading this page

  • Trigger: a reputable outlet publishes a Forever PvP class ranking, or a full live PvP season completes with participation and matchup data.
  • Then: the ranking is cited to its author with the date, and any ranking of our own must state its methodology, its gear level and its sample size in advance — the standard already published in advance on the Classes page.
  • Until then: a per-class list of verified mechanics, not a tier list. A reader who wants to pick a class now should pick the role and toolkit they will enjoy, which is also what the guide sites say when they are being straight (C200).

Druid Guide-reported, may change

Druid changes with battleground relevance. Source: Icy Veins Druid Class Overview (Meyra and Voulk), last updated 15 Sep 2026, read 18 Sep 2026.
Published changeWhy it matters in a battlegroundEvidence
Nature's Grasp is baseline with a 100% chance to entangle the next enemy that hits you. A guaranteed root on any attacker, available to every Druid without a talent investment — directly relevant to flag carrying and peeling. Guide Icy Veins
Berserk, new, 3-minute cooldown, 15 seconds. Cat: doubles combo-point-generator crit chance. Bear: removes Mangle's cooldown, hits 3 targets. Fear immunity while active. A scheduled immunity to Fear is one of the few published counters to Warlock and Warrior control. Guide Icy Veins
Tiger's Fury: 15% physical damage for 6s, 30s cooldown, costs no energy; pairs with King of the Jungle for 60 energy. A short, repeatable burst window rather than a resource trade. Guide Icy Veins
Bleeds and heal-over-time effects can critically strike; Omen of Clarity is baseline and procs from spells and heals. Changes how crit gear is valued for both Feral pressure and Restoration sustain. Guide Icy Veins
Swiftmend no longer consumes the heal-over-time effect; Wild Growth added; Gift of the Earthmother cuts the global cooldown of Rejuvenation, Swiftmend and Wild Growth to 1 second. Faster reactive healing under focus fire, which is the healer's whole job at a node. Guide Icy Veins
Furor reworked: power-shifting no longer generates extra energy. The Classic Feral tempo technique is functionally removed; Icy Veins calls this a loss of long-term tempo. Guide Icy Veins
Shapeshift forms allow consumable usage; abilities scale with weapon damage; Entangling Roots indoor castable; Nature's Grasp shapeshift castable. Feral Bear and Cat forms can now use potions and consumables while shifted, and attacks scale from equipped weapon damage rather than purely unscaled form attack power (C172). Guide Wowhead Feral Guide

Races: Night Elf and Tauren (Classic), plus Skyborne (both factions). Night Elf Shadowmeld and Quickness, Tauren War Stomp and Plainsrunning, and Skyborne Walk on Air are the racials with stated mobility or control value — see the racial table. Open question: whether the Furor change materially weakens Feral in open-field play. Nobody has tested it above level 20. Ledger C111.

Hunter Guide-reported, may change

Hunter changes with battleground relevance. Source: Icy Veins Hunter Class Overview (Impakt), last updated 15 Sep 2026, read 18 Sep 2026.
Published changeWhy it matters in a battlegroundEvidence
Traps are usable in combat. A material functional change for Hunter PvP: Freezing and Explosive Trap stop requiring a disengage-and-reset. Guide Icy Veins
Abilities no longer clip Auto Shot, though you still must stand still to shoot. Removes the shot-timer skill tax while keeping the stand-still constraint that defines Hunter positioning. Guide Icy Veins
Pets scale with the hunter's stats. Pet damage and durability stop falling off as gear improves, so the pet remains a real pressure and body-block tool. Guide Icy Veins
Lone Wolf: early Marksmanship talent, 20% damage with no active pet. A published trade-off — damage against losing the pet's control and body. Both sides of that trade are testable at launch. Guide Icy Veins
Sniper Shot: Marksmanship capstone, 4-second cast, described as a large burst option. A 4-second cast is interruptible and immobile; it suits defended positions, not a scrum. Guide Icy Veins
Survival is redesigned as a melee specialization (Savage Strikes, Strider Kick, Predator's Edge, improved traps). An entirely new archetype with no Classic precedent — it cannot be predicted from Classic experience. Guide Icy Veins
Human is a new Hunter race. Icy Veins states Will to Survive breaks stuns and does not share a cooldown with the standard PvP trinket. If accurate at launch, that is two stun-breaks per fight. We flag it: the trinket sharing rule is the guide's statement, not Blizzard's. Guide Icy Veins

Flagged for review: the “does not share a cooldown with the PvP trinket” line implies a PvP trinket exists in Forever. No PvP vendor inventory has been published (see unpublished PvP facts), so treat that as an assumption carried over from Classic, not a Forever fact. Ledger C112, C120.

Mage Guide-reported, may change

Mage changes with battleground relevance. Source: Icy Veins Mage Class Overview (Wrdlbrmpft), last updated 13 Sep 2026, read 18 Sep 2026.
Published changeWhy it matters in a battlegroundEvidence
Fingers of Frost: chill effects have a 30% chance to treat your next 2 spells as if the target were frozen, for 15 seconds. Shatter-level crit and Ice Lance damage without an actual root — a mobile burst window that Classic Frost never had. Guide Icy Veins
Ice Lance: instant, 300% increased damage to a frozen target. An instant finisher that pairs with every freeze and with Fingers of Frost. Guide Icy Veins
Shatter reaches its 50% crit bonus against frozen targets at 3 points and no longer requires Improved Frost Nova. Frees talent points that Classic Frost had to spend, widening build options. Guide Icy Veins
Improved Blizzard nerfed: chill is 45% for 1.5 seconds, down from 65% for 2 seconds in Classic. Area slow pressure on a node is weaker than Classic players will expect. A rare published nerf worth planning around. Guide Icy Veins
Hot Streak stacks to 3, each stack cutting Pyroblast's cast time by 25% (to 4.5 / 3 / 1.5 seconds). Makes a Fire burst window reachable without a six-second cast, which is the reason Fire was unplayable in Classic PvP. Guide Icy Veins
Frostfire Bolt: damages, slows and applies a damage-over-time effect, using whichever school the target resists less. A slow that ignores single-school resistance gear. Guide Icy Veins

Races: Gnome, Human, Undead, Troll, plus new Orc Mage and Alliance Skyborne Mage. Icy Veins notes Orc Axe Specialization is irrelevant to Mages because Mages cannot wield axes — a useful example of a racial that looks strong on a table and is not. Open question: whether the Blizzard chill nerf outweighs the Fingers of Frost addition. Ledger C113.

Paladin Official — full changelist published

The only class Blizzard has documented in full. Every row here is Blizzard's own text from the Deep Dive recap, which is why this is the one profile that does not carry a “may change” caveat beyond normal beta tuning.

Paladin changes with battleground relevance. Source: Blizzard, World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 13 Sep 2026.
Published changeWhy it matters in a battlegroundEvidence
Blizzard's stated limits: Paladins still lack an interrupt, still cannot slow targets the way some other classes can, and still reach their taunt through the Seal and Judgment system. Blizzard naming a class's gaps in writing is the strongest published evidence on this page. A Paladin cannot stop a cast or kite a runner. Official Blizzard
Voice of Truth (Holy): temporary immunity to Silence and Interrupts. A published counter to caster lockdown, on the spec most likely to be focused at a node. Official Blizzard
Holy Strike at level 6: instant weapon strike dealing Holy damage, 12-second cooldown. Seals are no longer consumed by Judgment. Instant damage that is not blocked by school lockouts on physical attacks, and one less reason to stop and recast mid-fight. Official Blizzard
Consecration baseline at level 20, with high damage and threat on the first four targets. A baseline area effect: relevant to stealth detection around a flag and to contesting a capture point. Official Blizzard
Templar's Bulwark (Protection): absorb shield based on maximum health, interacting with Forbearance. Absorb-based survivability on top of the Classic bubble, with the Forbearance trade-off preserved. Official Blizzard
Retribution: Vindication reduces enemy Attack Power while raising the Paladin's own; Instrument of the Law makes Holy Wrath instant; Twist of Light supports Seal twisting without a swing-timer addon. An instant Holy Wrath is a usable stun against Undead and Demons only; Vindication is a published damage-reduction debuff. Official Blizzard
Undead Paladin is one of the six new race/class combinations, bringing Paladin to the Horde. The 16 September mount datamine shows the Forsaken Charger — the Undead Paladin racial mount — in the beta client (C187, may change), alongside the official level-60 epic mount quest in the Found Photos recap. Priest Guide-reported, may change

Priest changes with battleground relevance. Source: Icy Veins Priest Class Overview (Rainy), last updated 16 Sep 2026, read 18 Sep 2026.
Published changeWhy it matters in a battlegroundEvidence
Fear Ward is no longer Dwarf-only — it is baseline for every Priest race. Removes the single biggest race-lock in Classic Priest PvP. Every Priest now has a fear pre-empt. Guide Icy Veins
Devouring Plague is no longer Undead-only; Shadow Word: Death is a new Shadow spell. Shadow pressure and a finisher become race-independent. Guide Icy Veins
Shadowform now blocks healing spells rather than Holy spells; +10% Shadow damage, −50% Shadow mana cost, +100% Shadow crit damage, −15% physical damage taken. A standing 15% physical mitigation is a defensive statistic, not a damage one — it changes how melee trades against a Shadow Priest. Guide Icy Veins
Race-specific Priest spells persist: Dwarf gains Chastise (a root against Humanoids) in place of Fear Ward; Human trades Desperate Prayer for Divine Grace (heals a target below 50% health and removes Weakened Soul); Undead gains Dark Sacrifice (health into mana); Night Elf Elune's Grace now grants 50% dodge against melee and ranged. This is the one class where race choice still changes the spellbook, not just the racial bar. Guide Icy Veins
Divine Spirit is learned as a normal Priest spell rather than a Discipline talent. Matches Blizzard's statement that Divine Spirit is baseline. One of the few guide lines corroborated first-party. Official Blizzard
Gnome Priest is a new race/class combination, with two entirely new Priest racial spells: Confounding Flash (an area crowd-control effect that confuses up to 5 enemies) and Contingency Plan. An area crowd-control effect on a healer is a genuinely new tool in this game, and Escape Artist answers the root-and-snare chains healers are killed by. Official (combination) Guide (race-spells) BlizzardIcy Veins

Explicit gap: Icy Veins states Troll Priest race-specific spells were not confirmed from the BlizzCon demo. We leave that blank rather than filling it. Race note: Skyborne cannot be Priest; the playable Priest races are Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome, Undead and Troll. Ledger C115.

Rogue Guide-reported, may change

Rogue changes with battleground relevance. Source: Icy Veins Rogue Class Overview (Sellin), last updated 15 Sep 2026, read 18 Sep 2026.
Published changeWhy it matters in a battlegroundEvidence
Restless Blades: spending combo points reduces the cooldowns of Adrenaline Rush, Blade Flurry, Evasion, Sprint and Vanish by 2 seconds per point. Sprint and Vanish are the flag-carrier and escape buttons; making them cooldown-reducible is the most PvP-relevant Rogue change published. Guide Icy Veins
Cutthroat: Backstab has a chance to make the next Ambush usable outside stealth. Opener-level burst without re-stealthing, which changes how a Rogue re-engages after a failed kill attempt. Guide Icy Veins
Energy regenerates continuously instead of in two-second ticks. Removes tick-timing from every stun-lock sequence; the whole class plays at a different pace. Guide Icy Veins
Improved Kidney Shot now increases the Rogue's own damage against the target, scaling with points. A stun that is also a personal damage amplifier during the stun. Guide Icy Veins
Mutilate added (two combo points, bonus damage to poisoned targets); Venom is the Assassination capstone (+30% poison damage, +10% application chance, duration scaling with combo points). Faster combo generation means faster access to Kidney Shot and Cheap Shot chains. Guide Icy Veins
Hack and Slash gives per-weapon-type bonuses instead of forcing one weapon; Rogues can now use one-handed axes. Weapon availability stops constraining spec choice, which matters when gear is scarce at launch. Guide Icy Veins
From the beta spec guides (17 Sep): Subtlety notes energy regeneration is slow in Forever despite regenerating continuously; it names Human (Perception, sword crit) and Orc (stun resistance, Blood Fury) as the PvP race picks, recommends Engineering for PvP (grenades and gadgets) and First Aid bandaging after Gouge or Blind; Combat describes a two-button Sinister Strike/Eviscerate core with Kick as one of the few early interrupts and Sap/Blind arriving at later levels. The first guide-site race picks for PvP — corroborating this project's own synergy reasoning (C171) from a second angle — plus the Classic-era Engineering/First Aid consumable pattern restated for Forever. Guide Icy Veins SubtletyIcy Veins Combat

Counterplay to record: Human Perception detects stealth for 20 seconds and Paladin Consecration is baseline at 20 — both are published stealth counters. Whether they are sufficient is untested. Ledger C116.

Shaman Guide-reported, may change

Shaman changes with battleground relevance. Source: Icy Veins Shaman Class Overview (Seksixeny), last updated 17 Sep 2026, read 18 Sep 2026.
Published changeWhy it matters in a battlegroundEvidence
Earthbound: Earthbind Totem roots nearby targets for 5 seconds when cast. An instant area root on a 5-second window. Icy Veins calls it “incredibly powerful in PvP” — their words, attributed. Guide Icy Veins
Ghost Wolf: base cast reduced to 2 seconds; Improved Ghost Wolf can make it instant and usable everywhere at full investment. Instant travel form on a class that had none is a direct objective-running upgrade. Guide Icy Veins
Totemic Projection and Totemic Recall move totems up to 30 yards; Call of the Elements drops a full set with a 3-second cast. Totems stop being static liabilities when a fight moves across a capture point. Guide Icy Veins
Maelstrom Weapon: melee attacks stack a buff reducing Lightning Bolt's cast time and mana cost. Gives Enhancement instant ranged damage — relevant for finishing a fleeing flag carrier. Guide Icy Veins
Riptide is the Restoration capstone: a direct heal plus a heal-over-time that also boosts Chain Heal on its target by 25%. Instant-plus-HoT healing under pressure, the pattern healers need when they cannot stand still. Guide Icy Veins
Lightning Bolt max rank casts in 2.5 seconds and Chain Lightning is 0.5 seconds faster than Vanilla, baseline. Shorter casts are harder to interrupt and easier to land while moving between objectives. Guide Icy Veins
Dwarf Shaman brings Shaman to the Alliance; Horde Skyborne can also be Shaman. Dwarf Stoneform on a Shaman is new: a poison/bleed/disease cleanse plus 10% physical mitigation. Official Blizzard
From the beta Enhancement guide (17 Sep, Wordup): Maelstrom Weapon is confirmed carried forward to hybridise melee and spells; the talent tree targets Enhancement's Mana economy and ability cooldowns so Windfury Weapon can pair with the Windfury Totem group buff; more AoE via Fire Nova; and Improved Ghost Wolf is usable indoors. Indoors travel form and better mana economy extend Shaman mobility and sustain inside bases, tunnels and keeps — where battleground fights actually happen. Icy Veins rates the spec's early performance “tentatively average”, their words, subject to beta iteration. Guide Icy Veins Enhancement

Faction note: Shaman is no longer Horde-only, which changes the faction-balance argument that shaped Classic battlegrounds. Blizzard has separately said the PvP ruleset uses Season-of-Discovery-style faction balance. Ledger C117.

Warlock Guide-reported, may change

Warlock changes with battleground relevance. Source: Icy Veins Warlock Class Overview (Crix), last updated 15 Sep 2026, read 18 Sep 2026.
Published changeWhy it matters in a battlegroundEvidence
Demons gain a Move To command with 100-yard range. Icy Veins explicitly calls this a major improvement for precise positioning “especially in PvP” — a Felhunter can be sent to a caster without the Warlock following. Guide Icy Veins
Felhunter retains Spell Lock; demons scale with the Warlock's stats; Succubus was the strongest damage pet in the tested build; Voidwalker generated extreme threat. Spell Lock remains the class's interrupt, and pet scaling means it stays relevant at 60. Guide Icy Veins
Bane of Agony and Bane of Doom are no longer Curses. A Warlock can run a Bane and a Curse (for example Curse of the Elements) on the same target at once — more simultaneous pressure and debuff coverage. Guide Icy Veins
Damage-over-time effects can critically strike; Pandemic raises DoT crit damage by up to 100%. Fundamentally changes Affliction's damage profile and the value of crit gear in a dot-and-run playstyle. Guide Icy Veins
Improved Drains: the Drain Soul bonus is tripled against enemies below 20% health. A published execute window on a channelled spell. Guide Icy Veins
Spellstone and Firestone reworked into weapon oils (1% haste and spell power, or 1% crit and crit damage), no longer occupying the wand slot. A free wand slot and a permanent self-buff, which is a small but real gearing change. Guide Icy Veins
Haste does not currently affect DoTs or drain channels in the tested build. A stat-weight caveat flagged by the source itself. Re-check at each beta build before making gear decisions. Guide Icy Veins
Troll Warlock is a new race/class combination. Berserking on a caster is a published 10% cast-speed cooldown. Official Blizzard

Cross-reference: Blizzard named Fear as a form of crowd control that “still matters”, and Warlocks are the class built around it — while Undead Will of the Forsaken now only removes Fear without granting immunity afterwards. Those two published facts interact directly. Ledger C118.

Warrior Guide-reported, may change

Warrior changes with battleground relevance. Source: Icy Veins Warrior Class Overview (Abide), last updated 13 Sep 2026, read 18 Sep 2026.
Published changeWhy it matters in a battlegroundEvidence
Recklessness, Retaliation and Shield Wall no longer share cooldowns. Shield Wall is 60% damage reduction for 12 seconds; both Shield Wall and Retaliation sit on 15-minute cooldowns. The Classic penalty of locking out your defensive by using your offensive is gone — the single biggest published Warrior PvP change. Guide Icy Veins
Spearing Strike: 15 Rage, 40% weapon damage, +80% against Giants, Dragonkin or mounted targets, and forcibly dismounts mounted targets. A published dismount tool. In a game where mounts are expensive and travel is slow, dismounting a runner is an objective-denial ability. Guide Icy Veins
Bloodthirst deals 35% attack power (down from 45%) but grants 10% increased movement speed. A damage nerf traded for a mobility buff — an explicit sign the class is being tuned for chasing, not just standing. Guide Icy Veins
Victory Rush restores 10% maximum health after a qualifying kill, on a 30-second cooldown. Self-sustain between kills in a battleground, where kills are frequent. Guide Icy Veins
Thunder Clap works in Defensive Stance, slows enemy attack speed by 20% and hits up to 4 targets; Shield Block blocks 2 attacks over 7 seconds on a 5-second cooldown. Area attack-speed slow plus near-permanent block uptime for anyone defending a node. Guide Icy Veins
Weaponmaster merges the weapon-specific talents; Bloodthrill procs Overpower from Rend targets without requiring a dodge; Mortal Strike remains the Arms capstone with its healing reduction. Healing reduction is still the reason Arms exists in PvP, and Overpower access no longer depends on the enemy dodging. Guide Icy Veins
Warrior can be every race, including Skyborne — the only class with no race restriction. Maximum racial flexibility: Orc Hardiness, Human Will to Survive, Dwarf Stoneform and Skyborne Walk on Air are all available to a Warrior. Guide Icy Veins

Note on weapon skill: Blizzard states weapon skill still exists but items give less of it per item, so weapon-skill gear is no longer automatically correct. Ledger C119.

Per-spec guides now exist for every class — and they stop at level 20. The class switcher on Icy Veins' Arms Warrior guide lists one Forever spec guide per class, so the reading list is no longer Warrior-only: Balance Druid, Beast Mastery Hunter, Frost Mage, Holy Paladin, Discipline Priest, Assassination Rogue, Elemental Shaman, Affliction Warlock, Arms, Fury and Protection Warrior. Read them as skill and rotation references for the beta level cap, not as PvP rankings: the Arms guide states it “covers leveling and PvE play through level 20”, that its advice is “starting recommendations based on the available WoW Forever trainer and talent information”, and that its scores “are early ratings based on the Level 20 toolkit and do not reflect end-game potential”. The 19 September session read the Warrior overview and the Arms guide in full and registered the rest of the switcher's URLs from that page; they are on the weekly watcher. Ledger C139.

Update, later on 19 September: Icy Veins finished its spec-guide wave on 17–18 September — the full set now includes Combat and Subtlety Rogue, Enhancement and Restoration Shaman, Demonology and Destruction Warlock, Feral and Restoration Druid, Protection and Retribution Paladin, and Beast Mastery Hunter (C182). This project has read Combat Rogue, Subtlety Rogue and Enhancement Shaman end to end (the PvP-relevant picks: see C183); the rest are tracked through Icy Veins' own changelog, which is on the weekly watcher. The Subtlety guide is the first to name PvP race picks and Engineering-for-PvP advice — attributed analysis, quoted as such on the Rogue profile.

What the talent calculator is and is not. Wowhead's Forever calculator now shows all nine classes with three 51-point trees, rebuilt from BlizzCon demo footage and event streams; Wowhead warns that rank scaling may be wrong and that the tool will be rebuilt from beta datamining. It is the only browsable build view that exists — and it is not evidence about level-60 PvP power. Ledger C138.

What is missing from every profile — and when it arrives

Not announced for any class Gap

  • Any PvP-specific tuning pass or class-balance statement for battlegrounds.
  • A PvP stat system (a resilience equivalent). Blizzard has said only that PvP armor “has stats on it”.
  • Honor gear itemisation, vendor inventories or prices — so no class can be gear-planned for PvP.
  • Diminishing-returns rules on crowd control in Forever.
  • Whether the Darkspear Islands battleground has a level bracket, and whether the Classic battlegrounds are in at launch.

Our reasoning, labelled as such. Ours The only published class facts with unambiguous battleground meaning today are mobility, control, control-break and immunity lines — Druid Berserk's fear immunity, Shaman's talent-enabled instant Ghost Wolf and Earthbind root, Mage's frozen-target burst, Rogue's cooldown-reducible Vanish and Sprint, Warrior's dismount, Warlock's pet Move To, Paladin's stated lack of an interrupt or slow, and the racial cleanses (Will to Survive, Stoneform, Escape Artist, Hardiness, Will of the Forsaken). Damage tuning, gear scaling and team composition are not measurable before live level-60 play, and we will not estimate them. Ledger C121.
