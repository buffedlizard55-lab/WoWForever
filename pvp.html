PvP in World of Warcraft: Forever

Forever keeps Classic's faction war and adds a seasonal Honor ladder with 14 ranks per patch cycle. This page lists exactly what Blizzard and the dev interviews have confirmed, separates it from what is merely implied, and names every PvP number that is still unpublished.

Short answer for “what is the best PvP class?”

No verified answer exists yet, and we will not invent one. Beta is capped at level 20, there is no rated ladder to measure, the rank reward list exists only as a datamine (thresholds and prices are unpublished), and Blizzard has published a full class changelist for one class (Paladin) out of nine. Our per-class PvP profiles publish everything that is known per class, and the Classes page gives you the verified pieces that decide battleground value and a dated plan for when real rankings become possible.

Honor and rewards

What is confirmed

  • 14 ranks per patch cycle, resetting with the cycle. WH/Kotaku
  • Honor is the progression currency, earned in battlegrounds and PvP-related content. Kotaku
  • PvP armor awarded through the track has stats, so PvP gear is a real gearing path alongside dungeons and raids. WH/Kotaku
  • Seasonal structure means each content cycle brings a fresh ladder rather than a permanent one. Q&A
  • Honor gain is tuned so Alterac Valley is not the default best farm. Q&A

What is not published

  • Exact Honor per kill, per objective or per battleground, and any weekly caps.
  • Rank-by-rank thresholds, decay rules, or whether decay exists at all.
  • PvP vendor inventories and prices, including weapons — the rank reward list below is datamined, but what each item costs, and where it is bought, is not published.
  • Whether rank titles (Grand Marshal / High Warlord) return.
  • Whether battleground marks are used, and how the new Darkspear Islands reputation interacts with the track.
  • Any PvP-specific stat system beyond “armor with PvP stats” (for example resilience-style mechanics).

Recorded in the ledger as C029–C030 with status Not announced.

Flagged. Boosting and gold sites are currently advertising PvP rank services with specific numbers attached, and at least one lists a multi-thousand-dollar “Rank 14 pre-order” that pays out only if Blizzard ships a Classic honor ladder. Battlegrounds: confirmed versus implied

This is the single most important PvP distinction on the site, because it decides what you can plan to play on 4 November.

Confirmed in writing

Blizzard's written recaps name Darkspear Islands — 15 vs 15, control points plus Arathi-Basin-style flag capture — and list “PvP updates” among the content that follows launch. The Q&A liveblog records that Honor gains are being adjusted so that sitting in Alterac Valley is not the best option, which is a tuning remark, not a roster.

New this session: Blizzard's own beta article states that “additional zones, dungeons, and the new battleground will open for testing as the beta continues”. That is first-party confirmation that at least one new battleground is planned to be tested before launch — as a future statement, not a report that it is playable now — and it still does not name the classic three, nor say whether the beta battleground is Darkspear Islands.

Official BlizzardBeta articleWH/Kotaku

Said in an interview, not yet in writing

Whether Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin and Alterac Valley are in the launch roster is not stated in any Blizzard recap we could read, and Blizzard's beta article says only “the new battleground”. The strongest evidence is a developer's spoken answer: lead Classic designer Tim Jones told Kotaku, “We're excited for people to jump into new battlegrounds like the Darkspear Islands to supplement the original Vanilla battlegrounds that people will be able to jump into.” That is press-class evidence that the original maps remain; it names none of them, and it is not a launch list.

Press: developer quote Roster not announced KotakuQ&A liveblog

Why some third-party pages list WSG / AB / AV as confirmed

Several aggregation sites state that “the original Battlegrounds return” with no Blizzard citation, and some additionally describe “arena strategy” for a game in which no arena has ever been mentioned. Those pages are registered in our source list with tier community and are never used as authority here. If Blizzard publishes a battleground roster, this section will be updated with the primary link and the claim will move from pending to official.

Datamined, 16 September — the battleground's two reputations Datamine The beta client carries two opposed reputation records for the battleground's setting: the Darkspear Raiders, “Loyal to the troll nation” and battling “the Theramore Expeditionary Force for control of their ancestral Darkspear Islands”, and the Theramore Expeditionary Force, whose description mirrors it from the Alliance side. Twelve further new factions are in the same datamine (Guardians of Hyjal, Windshapers, High Order, Powderfuse, The Watchers, Brotherhood of the Horse, Bolder'ok Clan, Earthen Ring, Nightclaw Druids, Barkskin Burrow, Cenarion Scouts, Kirin Tor). No reputation reward, turn-in or quartermaster is in the data, and Wowhead's caveat is that “not all reputations may be present when we finally enter the game”. Ledger claim C186. Wowhead datamine

One step before that: in the Sportskeeda interview a Blizzard designer did say “I would love to have a Caverns of Time-themed battleground”. That is a personal wish, given with the caveat that it is long-term and “nothing to be promising”. It is recorded in the ledger as Not announced (C140) so that no future version of this page — or anyone reading it — turns an interview wish into a roadmap entry. Sportskeeda interview

What is being watched, and when it lands

  • The new battleground is expected inside the beta. Blizzard's own beta article says further zones, dungeons and the new battleground open during the test; nothing has confirmed it is open yet. Blizzard Progress is tracked on the Beta log.
  • Class balance is expected to move during the test. The developers said the beta is where they will “look at class balance now that some classes have new spells” and “make adjustments where necessary”, so every class profile on this site is pre-beta material (C151, C152). IGN Nordic
  • Measurement tooling is thin. With no combat-log parser carrying a Forever build, the supported path is the in-game meter — see the add-on tracker.
  • A ranking still waits for a season. No tier, grade or PvP score is published before a full season has produced participation, matchup, reward and tuning evidence (project decision, Work plan).

How to prepare without guessing

Do these (they are supported by verified mechanics)

  • Pick the PvP ruleset at character creation if you want open-world conflict — switching later needs a character transfer, and Blizzard has only described one such path (a dead Hardcore character moving out).
  • Choose your race with the reworked racials in mind: every race now has two active and two passive abilities, and some changed sharply (Undead lost post-trance immunity on Will of the Forsaken; Dwarf Stoneform now reduces Physical damage).
  • Plan for merged hit and crit: hybrid PvP sets get simpler, and Blizzard explicitly names Retribution and Enhancement as beneficiaries.
  • Expect Honor to be a gear path, not a side activity — the reward track hands out armor with stats, and the datamined rank list puts a trinket at rank 2, armour upgrades from rank 8 and weapons at rank 14.
  • If you sell consumables: combat potions are gated behind PvP rank 5 in the datamine, which creates a PvP-side demand curve for potions as well as a raid-side one.
  • Level a class you can play every cycle: the ladder resets each patch cycle, so the long-term investment is your character and profession base, not a one-off rank.

Don't do these (they assume unpublished facts)

  • Choosing a class from a level-60 tier list. None can be verified.
  • Buying “rank carry” or “BiS” services before Honor prices, rank thresholds and item stats exist.
  • Assuming arenas, rated brackets or classic titles will exist at launch.
  • Assuming a specific battleground list beyond Darkspear Islands — and treating an interview wish (a Caverns of Time battleground) as a plan.
  • Building PvP addons around automation: computational marking/communication addons will be restricted.

PvP season 1 dates: Blizzard has not published a start date for a PvP season. The nearest verified anchor is the 9 December 2026 raid unlock, and Wowhead's estimate of a “PvP season refresh” in a later spring window. Treat any specific season start date as unverified.
