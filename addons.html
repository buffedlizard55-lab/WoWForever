Add-ons & tooling · snapshot 2026-09-19

Add-on and tooling compatibility, dated

Gold-making depends on tooling, and tooling depends on the addon API. This page records, with a date and a link, which add-ons declare a Forever build, what Blizzard has actually said about the API, and what is still missing. Every row is re-checked each session; nothing here is copied from another site's compatibility list.

The finding that changed this page.

Auctionator release 337 (17 September 2026) declares “Supported Classic Forever patch1.60.1”. It is the first auction-house add-on this project has verified as Forever-capable, and it arrived on the day the beta opened. TradeSkillMaster, Questie and Details! all still list no Forever build. Wago listingTSM listing

A version list is tooling evidence, not proof that the add-on works in the live client. Treat “declares support” and “works on 4 November” as two different statements — see what is still unknown.

What Blizzard has said about the addon API

First-party statements and one reproduced Blizzard message. The Discord statement is cited as Wowhead's reproduction, never as a Blizzard web page (C106).
StatementWho said itEvidence
Forever “shares Mainline WoW's UI architecture, including the vast majority of APIs available in 12.1.5”, and the addon disarmament changes from Midnight — “including secrets” — are active in Forever. The 12.1.0 AuraContainer/AuraButton changes are available too. Blizzard, posted in the WoW UI Discord and reproduced by Wowhead. Press Wowhead
Every Forever add-on has to adapt to the new API: Classic-built add-ons will likely need to be remade, while current Midnight add-ons port over more easily. Blizzard, same statement; Wowhead's reading of the consequence. Press Wowhead
Addons are supported under modern WoW's rules: no computational automation of marking or communicating, and no one-button rotation; a built-in damage meter is in, with a cooldown manager planned. Blizzard developers in the Kotaku and MMORPG interviews. Press KotakuIcy Veins

Read together, these statements explain the pattern in the table above: add-ons must be rebuilt against a modern-era API, so the fastest movers are the projects already maintaining a Mainline build. Auctionator matches that shape; the TSM, Questie and Details! listings do not yet.

What this means for gold-making Our reasoning

  • The first auction-house tool exists, and it is not TSM. Auctionator's Forever build means a player can scan and compare auctions on 4 November without waiting for TradeSkillMaster. What it does not do is produce an automated price feed for this site: the market log stays a human observation log by design (data/market-log.csv), and the tool that fills the log is the same tool a reader can install.
  • TSM-shaped workflows are still blocked. Crafting cost, shopping operations and accounting are the parts of TSM most used by gold-makers, and none of them are available for Forever today. The earlier blanket position recorded for TSM alone (C093) remains true for TSM specifically.
  • Quest routing is a manual cost. With no Questie build, leveling and gathering routes through the new zones have to be learned rather than displayed — which is also why the site publishes quest counts and zone lists rather than route maps.
  • Restrictions are a market factor. Restricted computational add-ons mean fewer players running automated decision tools, so knowledge and attention keep more of their value. That is stated here as reasoning, not as a measured effect.

What this means for PvP measurement Our reasoning

  • No combat-log parser means no objective performance data we can publish yet. Details! has no Forever build, and the built-in meter is the supported path. Any damage or healing figures this site ever publishes must say which tool produced them.
  • No rotation assistance means class advice stays advice. The Class PvP profiles describe published mechanics; they cannot claim a measured performance edge, and the tier-list hold is unchanged — a full season must produce participation, matchup, reward and tuning evidence first.
  • What would change the picture: a Forever build of a meter or parser, a rated ladder, or Blizzard publishing Honor thresholds and vendor tables. Each of those is watched on this page and in the Beta log.

How this table stays honest

  • Every listing is a registered source. Wago and CurseForge pages are in data/sources.js with the tooling evidence class, so a page cannot cite an add-on listing that no one registered and re-checked.
  • They are watched automatically. The weekly source-watch Action fingerprints every registered URL — including these listings — and opens an issue when one changes.
  • The class is deliberately narrow. A tooling source can support a claim about software versions, and nothing else. Game facts still need Blizzard, press or datamine evidence.
  • Dates are printed, not implied. Each row carries the version number and the date it was read, because an add-on listing changes without notice.
