Best in Slot in WoW Forever: status, framework, and the tracker we will fill

“Find BiS guides for all classes” is a hard requirement of this project. It is also, today, impossible to satisfy honestly — and Blizzard did that on purpose. This page explains the block with primary sources, publishes the verified gearing framework you can already act on, and gives you the per-class tracker format that the item data will be poured into the moment it exists.

BiS status: blocked by design, not by laziness.

Blizzard is obfuscating items. In the MMORPG interview, the developers said players “will not see item stats until it drops for you or on your server”, there will be no Dungeon Journal, and they expect the first raid week to be a “Wild West” before community BiS lists form. Wowhead summary of the MMORPG interview

On top of that: the beta tops out at level 20 (30 later), the 55–60 dungeons and both raids are unreachable, and every dungeon drop is being re-itemised. Any level-60 BiS list published today — for any class — cannot be verified from the available evidence, so it is not published here. The clearest single proof arrived this session: Wowhead has datamined raid tier set appearances for all nine classes, and states plainly that no items are attached to them yet. Wowhead datamine A secondary guide makes the same scope limit explicit: its Arms Warrior guide “covers leveling and PvE play through level 20”, its advice is “starting recommendations based on the available WoW Forever trainer and talent information”, and its scores “are early ratings based on the Level 20 toolkit and do not reflect end-game potential”. Icy Veins Arms Warrior guide (Quotation corrected this session: a shorter paraphrase was previously attributed to the same guide without a checkable sentence behind it. The three quoted fragments above were read on the page.)

Stat rules that change every comparison

  • One hit stat, one crit stat. Spell, melee and ranged hit were merged, and spell, melee and ranged crit were merged. Hybrid gear stops being a compromise — Blizzard names Retribution and Enhancement as beneficiaries.
  • Bonus healing now carries one third as much bonus damage. Healers can quest and PvP without a second set.
  • Caster weapons grant spell damage and healing, so a weapon upgrade matters for casters the way it always did for Warriors and Rogues.
  • Weapon skill is worth less per item, so weapon-skill pieces are no longer automatic winners.
  • New stats reduce the chance your attacks are dodged or parried — relevant for tanks holding threat and for melee in PvP.
  • Trinkets are pushed toward situational effects (example: Worgenbane Talisman), plus environment and creature-type effects.

All six bullets: Blizzard Deep Dive recap. Ledger claims C060–C064.

Why the tracker shows no item names at all

Because two independent blockers apply: (1) item stats are hidden until a drop is recorded on a realm, and (2) every dungeon drop has been re-itemised, so Classic item names are not a reliable proxy. Publishing names now would create exactly the kind of unverifiable content this project exists to prevent. The tracker's rows are therefore intentionally empty but structured, and the verification procedure below is the contract for filling them.

Appearance rules change how loot is rolled before any BiS list exists (C210). A bind-on-pickup Uncommon or Rare item that drops in a dungeon grants its appearance to every eligible looter when it is looted, so nobody has to roll against an upgrade for a look. Epic raid drops are the exception: the appearance is granted only to the player the item binds to, which keeps raid appearances scarce and organised loot decisions in the raid's hands. Transmog itself is opt-out — a transmogrifier can switch it off, Classic Mode switches it off automatically at first launch, and low-level characters cannot apply high-level appearances until level 60 removes the restriction.

How each item is verified before it appears on this site

  1. Locate the item in a Forever database. Wowhead's Forever namespace is separate from Classic, for example /forever/item=226404/tarnished-undermine-real. Record the item ID and permalink.
  2. Confirm the stats on the live page. Screenshot or quote the stat block, and note the date. Never copy a stat block from a third-party summary.
  3. Confirm the source. Dungeon boss, quest, vendor, profession recipe or raid boss — with the dungeon's official level band attached (for example, Blackmaw Hold 55–60) so readers know when it is obtainable.
  4. Check the version filters on the drop list. Forever item pages aggregate data from other game versions (the worked example on this page carries Season of Discovery difficulty filters and vanilla raid bosses), so a drop source is only accepted when it is confirmed by Forever-specific material. Logged as irregularity I-8, claim C110.
  5. Confirm the phase. Launch (4 Nov) or raid phase (9 Dec) or later. Any item from a raid is not a pre-raid BiS item and must not be labelled as one.
  6. Note the caveats. Datamined-only items get a Datamine tag and are never presented as farmable facts.
  7. Add the claim row. Every item gets a claim ID in data/claims.js so a reviewer can trace it back to the URL and date.
  8. Re-verify on every content patch. Item changes after a patch are logged as diffs, not silent edits.
