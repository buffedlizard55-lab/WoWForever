Scope and rules of this project

  • No hallucinations. Every factual sentence is tied to a registered source; the ledger is machine-readable in data/claims.js.
  • No filler rankings. We do not publish a tier list, a BiS list or a gold-per-hour number that we cannot verify.
  • Attribution, not copying. Wowhead and Icy Veins are linked and credited for their work; we summarise and point, we do not rehost their content.
  • Flagged sources stay flagged. Gold-selling and boosting sites appear only in the irregularity log, never as authority.
  • Updates are dated. Every page states its snapshot date; a fact that changes keeps its ID so reviewers can diff it.
  • Shop facts are labelled as shop facts. Pre-order prices and contents come from the shop listing, not from gameplay, and are tagged accordingly.
