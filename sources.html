Flagged sources (recorded, not used)

We keep a record of sources we examined and rejected. This is deliberate: when a reader asks “why didn't you use site X?”, the answer is on the record with a date.

  • Gold/boosting-market sites — publish confident Forever tier lists, BiS lists and PvP rank numbers with no verifiable basis, and many sell carries or currency. Registered with tier community, excluded from every claim about the game, and cited only as evidence that the irregularity exists (ledger rows C100–C101). No link is published to them.
  • Unofficial “Forever” guide domains — a mix of honest “not announced” statements and unsupported claims (for example, arena strategy for a game with no announced arena). Used only as negative examples.
  • Community forum threads — useful for spotting questions, never used as evidence. A Blizzard-forum player post is a player post, not a design document.
  • Unattributed aggregator sites — pages that repeat a date or number with no link to Blizzard or to whoever first reported it. If a claim cannot be traced to a first party, it does not enter the ledger.

Full list with publishers and the reason for rejection: data/sources.js → entries whose IDs begin with flag-.
