Forever roadmap

Blizzard has only dated three things: the beta window, the global launch, and the first raid unlock. Everything after that exists as “later” in Blizzard's own words and as estimates on Wowhead's roadmap. This page keeps those two categories apart, because conflating them is how false roadmaps spread.

Legacy: the account-wide reward track Datamine

Legacy is Forever's long-term progression: you complete achievement-style goals across the whole game and spend the points on permanent, account-wide perks. The reward track below was datamined by Wowhead, which marks it as subject to change.

Datamined Legacy reward track (Wowhead, 17 September 2026). Wowhead notes the final versions will likely be adjusted before launch.
Legacy pointsRewardType
15Replica Ironforge Air RifleToy — you and other owners can stun each other; it keeps score
25Spectral Bear CubCompanion pet
40Spectral Bear TabardCosmetic tabard
55Reins of the Spectral BearEpic mount, 100% increased speed

All four are claimed from Innkeeper Wiley in Ratchet. Sources: Wowhead — PvP and Legacy System Rewards, Wowhead — Legacy System Overview. Ledger claim C105. The perk trees themselves (Professions, Adventure, Resourcefulness; 16 points spendable at launch out of up to 65 earnable) come from Blizzard's Deep Dive recap and Wowhead's earn-methods datamine.

Why the mount matters: riding training is the main gold sink and mounts are not free at level 40, so a free epic mount at 55 Legacy points is the single largest convenience reward on the track — and a reason to check Legacy point sources early rather than late.

Launch-readiness checklist

Verified statements on the left, marketing assumption on the right. Correct as of the snapshot date.

  • Server rulesets: Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, Hardcore (Hardcore after launch) — no realm list, no cross-ruleset grouping.
  • Factions stay separate; no cross-faction grouping anywhere.
  • Launch is one global moment; no early-access window.
  • Access is via subscription or Game Time — the game is not a separate purchase.
  • Early Name Reservations are a perk of all three pre-order packs; the reservation window dates were not opened line-by-line for this snapshot, so treat specific dates as unverified.
  • Skyborne and Zephras Isle require at least the $29.99 Heroic Pack — the only pay-gated content documented so far.
  • “BiS lists are ready for launch” — impossible while items are obfuscated until they drop.
  • “Forever will have WoW Tokens / paid boosts” — Blizzard has said there are no plans for either (interview statement).
  • “Arena is coming” — nothing has been announced about arenas in Forever.

Sources: Deep Dive recap, Q&A liveblog, Warcraft Tavern, MMORPG interview via Wowhead.
