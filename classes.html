Classes in WoW Forever, and what “best for PvP” can honestly mean today

Players want a battleground tier list. What they can have on day one is the raw material a tier list is built from: every published class change, every reworked racial, and the itemization rules that decide how classes scale. This page publishes that material with sources, states plainly that no ranking is verifiable yet, and schedules the first honest ranking.

Why there is no verified tier list — in four facts.

1. The beta is capped at level 20 and rises to level 30 later in the test; the 55–60 dungeons and both raids are unreachable, so nobody has played Forever endgame. BlizzardWowhead beta guide
2. Blizzard has published a complete class changelist for one class (Paladin); the Deep Dive says “more class and race details are planned”. Blizzard
3. Wowhead's Forever class-guide category contains four guides at the time of checking (re-checked 2026-09-18) — three PvE tank guides and the racials guide. None of them is a PvP guide or a ranking. Wowhead
4. The PvP ladder has no published Honor rates, rank thresholds or reward tables, so nobody can measure what PvP performance even means in this game yet. WH/Kotaku

Everything a level-60 PvP tier list would need — season data, Honor economy, gear availability, damage on live targets — arrives at 4 November at the earliest, and realistically around the 9 December raid unlock.

And the picture is expected to move during the test. The developers said the beta is where they will “look at class balance now that some classes have new spells” and “make adjustments where necessary”, so every class fact on this page is pre-beta material. IGN Nordic What would upgrade a claim, and what has been observed so far (nothing), is tracked on the Beta observation log.

Our analysis: how to choose without a tier list Our reasoning

This section is our reasoning, built only from verified mechanics plus Blizzard's statement that class design stays adjacent to Classic. It is not a ranking, and we change it the moment real data appears. Where a line depends on a Classic pattern rather than a Forever statement, we say so.

Our analysis: how to choose without a tier list (our reasoning). Columns: Battleground job, Verified anchors that matter, What can be compared now — not a recommendation.
Battleground jobVerified anchors that matterWhat can be compared now — not a recommendation
Carry the flag / objective mobility Terrain and travel matter; Tauren Plainsrunning reaches up to +30% movement while moving; Night Elf Quickness adds run speed; Skyborne Walk on Air glides; mounts are gated behind expensive riding training, so early battlegrounds are played slower. Use Druid and Rogue only as Classic comparison points, not as a Forever ranking. The published movement tools to compare are Tauren Plainsrunning, Night Elf Quickness and Skyborne Walk on Air; no live role result exists.
Crowd control and fight control Blizzard explicitly names Polymorph, Banish, Fear and Root as tools that still matter, alongside Threat management; addons that automate marking/communication are restricted, so human coordination matters more. Compare the named tools on Mage and Warlock — Polymorph, Banish and Fear — and the Undead Fear interaction. The sources do not establish which class wins a matchup.
Front-line team fight Single-difficulty, no-flex design; 10–15 second solo kills; bonus healing now adds one-third damage, so support hybrids retain solo presence; Paladin keeps its bubble/heal kit and gains Voice of Truth. Compare the published Warrior and Paladin kits, plus Orc Hardiness and Blood Fury. These mechanics are useful inputs, not evidence that either class or racial is strongest.
Anti-hunter / stealth matchup value Human Perception detects stealth for 20 seconds; Night Elf Shadowmeld grants stealth while immobile; Gnome Escape Artist answers root/snare chains. Human, Night Elf and Gnome are comparison options because each has a published counter-tool; no matchup win rate or class ranking is available.
Long-term ladder investment The Honor track has 14 ranks and resets each patch cycle; PvP armor carries stats, so gear from PvP is a real progression path. No class recommendation is supportable before a full season. Choose a class and profession pairing for personal goals, then replace this row with observed participation, rewards and matchup evidence.

Do not treat the right-hand column as a tier list. It is a comparison inventory of published mechanics, not “strongest at level 60 in a live season.” The distinction matters: a class can be a Classic comparison point for a job and still be tuned differently in Forever — which is exactly what the beta cannot tell us yet.

What we will publish, and exactly when

Our ranking methodology (published in advance)

  1. Battleground roles, not duels. Objective play (flag carry, node defence, choke control, healing/dispels) weighted above 1v1 outcomes.
  2. Tag each data point with its origin: Blizzard text, official blue post, beta datamine, live server observation, or community report.
  3. Separate gear assumptions. A ranking is only meaningful relative to a stated gear level (pre-raid BiS, tier 1, Honor set).
  4. Minimum sample rule. No class moves tiers on fewer than a stated number of recorded battlegrounds or logged fights.
  5. Version-stamped. Every ranking states the game build, the date, and what changed since the previous version.

This methodology is ours; we publish it now so the first ranking we produce can be audited against it.

Earliest dates for real evidence

  • Now: racial/ability verification at levels 1–20 only.
  • ~1 Oct 2026: level 30 bracket opens in beta, widening the ability set for testing.
  • 4 Nov 2026: live level 60; first real duels, world PvP and battleground queue data.
  • 9 Dec 2026: raids open; PvP reward economy and gear comparisons become measurable.
  • After the first PvP season reports: the first tier list we would sign our name to.
